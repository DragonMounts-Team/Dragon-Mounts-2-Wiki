#Finding nests

Natural nests generate in the world and hold a dragon egg. Each species spawns its nests in specific biomes, listed below. Nests for the Ender, Enchant, Dark, Storm and Wither dragons do not generate; those eggs come from conversion or transformation (see Eggs and taming).
